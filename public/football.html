May 2011

The Genesis (UCL Final)

Your very first memory. Messi destroys Manchester United at Wembley, scoring a brilliant goal to secure the Champions League. You walked in right at the pinnacle of Pep Guardiola's legendary side.

June 2015

The Peak of MSN (The Treble)

Alongside Neymar and Luis Suárez, Messi wins the Treble. It is the peak of the most devastating attacking trio in football history, and—unbeknownst to you at the time—his final Champions League trophy with Barca.

April 2017

The Bernabéu Shirt Hold

Messi scores a 92nd-minute winner against Real Madrid, marks his 500th goal for Barcelona, and holds his shirt up to a silenced Santiago Bernabéu. The ultimate image of his club career.

July 2021

The International Curse Broken

After years of watching him suffer agonizing final defeats with Argentina, Messi finally lifts his first major international trophy, beating Brazil at the Maracanã to win the Copa América.

December 2022

Football, Completed

The cinematic masterpiece. Messi wins the World Cup in Qatar, scoring twice in the greatest final ever played, settling the GOAT debate forever and completing his storybook career.
